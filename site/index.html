Tessera

A calm, dense system for internal tools and data-heavy apps.

Tessera is a calm, dense system for product UI: internal tools and data-heavy web apps such as queues, records, dashboards and back-offices. Neutral gray surfaces do the work; one indigo accent marks what you can act on; status colours only ever mean status.

For developersInstall, mount components, type and theme them. For designersGet the Figma library, pick a theme, hand off. FoundationsContent, colour, type, space, states and icons. TokensEvery colour, type style, space, radius and shadow. 78 componentsLive previews with when to use, do and don't. ThemesFour accents and four typefaces, light and dark, all WCAG AA.
